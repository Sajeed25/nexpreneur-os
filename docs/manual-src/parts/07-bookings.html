Chapter 10Bookings, Calendar and Floor map

Booking is the heart of daily operations. Members book desks and rooms in a few taps; staff see everything at a glance; and the system makes sure two people can never book the same space at the same time.

10.1 Making a booking

Bookings › New booking
1 Locationtop-bar selector
2 Typedesk, room…
3 Whendate, from, to
4 Checkavailability
5 Choosea space
6 Reviewprice + GST
7 Confirmdone
  1. Where? Choose the location with the selector at the top of the page.
  2. What? Tap a type: Hot Desk, Dedicated Desk, Meeting Room, Private Office, Phone Booth or Event Space.
  3. When? Pick the date and the From and To times (every 30 minutes, 06:00 to 22:00).
  4. Press Check availability. You see every space of that type with its price, and unavailable ones greyed with an Unavailable tag.
  5. Tap the space you want. A Review card appears.
  6. Check the subtotal, GST 18% and total, then press Confirm booking.

Rules the system enforces

  • Bookings must fall between 06:00 and 22:00 and last at most 12 hours.
  • The start cannot be in the past, and the end must be after the start.
  • No double-booking. When you press Confirm the system locks the space for a moment and re-checks. If someone else took it a second earlier, you see “Sorry, that slot was just taken. Pick another time.”
  • Resources marked Reserved, Maintenance or Unavailable cannot be booked.
Payment for bookings

A confirmed booking shows the amount payable including GST, which is collected at reception. Bookings do not currently create an invoice by themselves; if you want a booking billed on a GST invoice, create an invoice (chapter 11) or use a membership plan that includes the hours.

Booking statuses

Confirmed and Cancelled are used today. The labels Pending, Completed and No Show exist for future use. A booking in the past simply stays Confirmed.

10.2 The three views

Bookings  (tabs: List · Calendar · Floor map)

List

A searchable table of bookings from 60 days ago to 60 days ahead: space, booked by, date and time, total, status. Members see only their own; staff see everyone’s. Future bookings have a Cancel button (a confirmation appears). Members can cancel only their own; reception and managers can cancel any, and the member is notified.

Calendar

Switch between Day, Week and Month. Use the arrows to move and Today to jump back. Each booking is a small block with the space name and time. In Month view each day shows up to three bookings and a “+N more” counter. Cancelled bookings are hidden.

Floor map

Every resource is a coloured tile, grouped by type, showing its status right now. Press a tile to open a side panel with the name, capacity, price, status, today’s bookings and a Book this… button.

10.3 Cancelling a booking

  1. Bookings › List, find the booking (search by space or person).
  2. Press Cancel and confirm.

The slot becomes free immediately and the person gets a “Booking cancelled” notification (noting if the front desk cancelled it). Past bookings cannot be cancelled.

10.4 Tips for front desk and staff

  • Use the Calendar › Day view each morning to see the day’s meetings.
  • If a room is being repaired, set it to Maintenance in Resources (chapter 6) so nobody books it; you do not need to cancel anything unless there are already bookings.
  • Members can book from their phone. If someone cannot, you can sign in and book for yourself, but bookings are always made for the signed-in person.
