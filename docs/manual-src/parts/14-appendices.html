Appendix BCalculation cheat sheet

Every number the system produces can be checked by hand. All amounts are kept in paise internally, so there are no rounding surprises.

IGST (other state)The full GST₹719.82
Invoice totalSubtotal − discount + GST₹3,999 − 0 + 719.82 = ₹4,718.82
Percent couponSubtotal × % ÷ 10010% of ₹3,999 = ₹399.90
Fixed couponThe amount, never more than the subtotal₹500 off ₹3,999
Hourly bookingHours × hourly price; GST added2 × ₹800 = ₹1,600 → ₹1,888
Daily bookingDaily price once, whatever the hours; GST added₹300 → ₹354
Renewal dateStart date + billing cycle (1, 3 or 12 months); month ends clamp31 Jan + 1 month = 28 Feb

Invoice balanceTotal − paid (refunds reduce “paid”)₹4,718.82 − ₹2,000 = ₹2,718.82
Refund limitPayment − already refunded₹4,718.82 − ₹1,000 = ₹3,718.82 left

Appendix CGlossary

TermMeaning
Active / Paused / CancelledStates of a membership: renewing, temporarily stopped, ended for good.
ArchiveHide something you no longer use without losing its history (plans, resources, locations).
Audit logThe recorded list of who did what and when.
Billing cycleHow often a plan is invoiced: monthly, quarterly or yearly.
CGST / SGST / IGSTCentral, State and Integrated GST. CGST + SGST apply within a state, IGST between states.
ChurnThe share of memberships that ended in a period.
CouponA code that gives a percentage or fixed rupee discount.
Cron / daily jobA task that runs by itself on a schedule (renewals and reminders).
GSTINThe 15-character GST registration number of a business.
ISTIndia Standard Time (UTC+5:30), used for all times.
LocationOne physical coworking space of your organisation.
Magic linkA one-time sign-in link sent by email.
Member / Team memberA customer, versus someone who works for you.
MembershipOne member on one plan.
MRR / ARRMonthly / annual recurring revenue.
OccupancyHow much of your bookable time is booked.
OrganisationYour whole business; it holds all locations, people and data.
PaiseOne hundredth of a rupee; amounts are stored in paise.
PlanWhat you sell: a price, a cycle and benefits.
QR codeThe square barcode a visitor shows at reception.
RazorpayThe service that takes online payments.
ResourceA bookable desk, room, office, phone booth or space.
SACServices Accounting Code printed on each invoice line.
SessionThe period you stay signed in (8 hours).
SMTPThe mail server the system uses to send email.
VoidCancel an unpaid invoice; it is kept but cannot be paid.
WebhookA message another service (Razorpay) sends to confirm something happened.

D.3 Daily routines

RoleA good routine
ReceptionMorning: Calendar › Day, Visitors › Today. During the day: check visitors in and out, add new members, answer Support. Evening: close finished requests.
FinanceMorning: Invoices (filter overdue), Payments. Record desk payments, send reminders personally where needed, review the daily job’s renewals. Weekly: Analytics export.
Community ManagerCheck Events registrations, post an announcement or feed update, reply to comments.
OwnerDashboard each morning; weekly Analytics and CRM review; monthly: review plans and prices, team roles and the audit log, take a database export.
