Chapter 7Members

The Members section is your customer list. Reception and managers use it to onboard people, keep details current, start plans and help with passwords.

7.1 The member list

Sidebar › Members
ColumnMeaning
MemberName and email. Click to open the member’s page.
CompanyLinked company (chapter 9) or the company name typed in.
PlanCurrent plan with a coloured status: active paused cancelled expired. “No plan” if none.
LocationHome location.
RenewalNext renewal date of an active plan.
OwesUnpaid invoice balance (shown to finance roles and managers).
StatusActive or Deactivated account.

Use the search box (name, email, company, plan, phone) and the two filters: Active / Deactivated / All, and Any plan / Plan active / paused / cancelled / No plan. The location selector narrows the list too.

7.2 Adding a member

Members › Add member

Owners, managers and reception can add members. The form has three parts.

Part 1: who they are

FieldNotes
Full name, EmailRequired. The email must not already have an account.
Phone, Job titleOptional. Job title shows in the member directory.
Company (linked)Pick an existing company from chapter 9. Invoices can then be billed to it.
Company nameFree text, if the company is not in the list.
Home locationThe location they mainly use. Defaults to the one chosen in the top selector.

Part 2: how will they sign in?

  • Email them a link to choose their own password (best): the member receives a welcome email with a Set my password button. The link works once and lasts 7 days. Available once email is set up.
  • Set a temporary password myself: you type one (at least 8 characters) and tell the member privately. They can change it later on their Profile.

Part 3: start a plan now (optional, finance roles and managers)

Choose a plan, start date and optional coupon. The system starts the plan, calculates the renewal date, issues the first GST invoice and emails it.

Sample data

Name: Kavya Verma · Email: kavya@codenest.example · Phone: +91 98765 43210 · Job title: Developer · Company: CodeNest · Home location: Nexpreneur Warangal

Sign-in: Email them a link · Plan: Flexi · ₹3,999 · Start: 5 Oct 2026 · Coupon: WELCOME10

Result: a green message “Kavya Verma was added and an invitation email was sent”, a link to invoice INV-2026-0003: ₹3,999.00 − 10% = ₹3,599.10 + GST ₹647.84 = ₹4,246.94, and a renewal date of 5 November 2026.

If something is missing

If the invitation email cannot be sent, the member is still created; the message tells you, and you can press Send password link on their page later. If the plan cannot be started (for example the coupon is invalid), the member is still created and the message explains why.

7.3 A member’s page

Click a name to open the member’s page. A header card shows contact details, job and company, home location, and badges for status and plan. Seven tabs follow:

TabWhat it holds
OverviewJoined date, last sign-in, amount owed, and the action buttons (7.4 to 7.6).
MembershipStart a plan; history of every plan with Pause, Resume and Cancel.
BookingsThe 15 latest bookings with space, time, price and status.
InvoicesThe 15 latest invoices with number, date, total, status. (Finance roles.)
ActivityThe recorded actions about this person (added, updated, password set…).
AccessDoor and desk access control. Coming soon.
DocumentsAgreements and ID documents. Coming soon.

7.4 Editing details and the member’s plan

  • Edit details (Overview tab): change name, email, phone, job title, company, home location. Changing the email moves their sign-in to the new address; it is recorded in Activity.
  • Start a plan (Membership tab): choose plan and start date, optional coupon. Same behaviour as 7.2.
  • Pause: stops renewals and invoices but keeps the history. Resume turns it back on.
  • Cancel: ends the plan permanently (a confirmation appears). To start again, start a new plan.

7.5 Deactivating and reactivating

On the Overview tab, Deactivate blocks sign-in immediately and signs the person out on the very next page they open. Nothing is deleted: invoices, bookings and history remain. Reactivate restores access. Use this when someone leaves.

7.6 Passwords for members

SituationWhat to do
New member, email set upChoose “Email them a link” when adding.
Member lost the invitationOverview › Send password link. A new 7-day link is emailed.
Member forgot their passwordThey can use Forgot password? themselves, or you can use Send password link.
Email is not set up, or they cannot receive emailOverview › Set a temporary password (8 to 72 characters) › Set password. They are signed out everywhere and use the new password.
Privacy

You can set or reset a password but you can never see a member’s existing password. Passwords are stored scrambled and cannot be read by anyone, including administrators.

7.7 Members versus staff

The Members section lists people with the Member role. Your own team (reception, finance, community, staff, managers) is created and managed in Settings › Team & roles (chapter 23). Anyone who signs up through the public page also starts as a member.
