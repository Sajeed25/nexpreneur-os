Chapter 14Visitors and check-in

Members invite guests ahead of time and share a QR code. When the guest arrives, reception checks them in with a few clicks, and the host is told straight away.

14.1 Inviting a visitor (members and staff)

Sidebar › Visitors › Invite visitor   or   Home › Invite Visitor
  1. Enter the visitor’s name, optional phone and optional email.
  2. Pick the date (today or later) and time, and write the purpose (“Client meeting”).
  3. Press Generate QR invitation.

A card appears with a QR code and a 32-character code. If you entered the visitor’s email and email is set up, they already received an email with the QR code inside it, plus the host name, date and time. If not, screenshot the QR or copy the code and send it yourself.

Sample data

Host: Priya Sharma  ·  Visitor: Karthik Naidu, +91 99887 76655, karthik@clientco.example  ·  Monday 5 Oct 2026, 14:00  ·  Purpose: Product demo

Code (example): 9f2c41ab7d3e4c05a1b8e6f0d27c9a34

Karthik’s email: “Priya Sharma has invited you to Nexpreneur. 2026-10-05 at 14:00 (Product demo). Show this QR code at reception.”

Under Today and Other days each invitation shows a status:

StatusMeaning
ExpectedInvited, not arrived.
Checked inOn site. The check-in time is shown.
Checked outLeft. Check-out time shown.
CancelledThe host cancelled.

Hosts can press Show QR to see it again, Resend to re-email the visitor, or Cancel while the status is Expected.

14.2 Checking visitors in and out (front desk)

Reception, staff, managers and owners see everyone’s invitations. Two ways to check a guest in:

By code

At the top of the page, type or paste the visitor’s code into Check in by code and press Check in.

From the list

Under Today, press Check in next to the guest. Later press Check out when they leave.

Sample data
VisitorHostTimeStatus
Karthik NaiduPriya Sharma14:00Checked in In 13:52
Meena JoshiAarav Reddy15:30Expected
Rahul PatelAnanya Rao11:00Checked out 11:05 – 12:20

When Karthik is checked in, Priya Sharma receives a notification: “Karthik Naidu has arrived”.

What the system checks

  • A code only works on the date of the visit. Anything else shows “This invitation is for 2026-10-06, not today”.
  • A guest cannot be checked in twice (“Karthik Naidu is already checked in”).
  • Only reception, staff, managers and owners can check people in or out; hosts cannot do it for their own guests.
  • A wrong code shows “No invitation found for that code”.
Tip

A camera scanner is not built in. Copy-paste the code, or have the visitor read it out. Most QR scanner apps on a phone copy the code to the clipboard, which you can paste into the box.

Chapter 15CRM: your sales pipeline

Every coworking space lives on enquiries. The CRM keeps each lead, what they want, who spoke to them and where they are in the sale. It is available to owners, super admins and location managers.

15.1 The board

Sidebar › CRM

Leads are cards in seven columns. The header shows the number of leads and the pipeline value: the total expected value of every lead that is not Lost.

15.2 Adding a lead

  1. Press New lead.
  2. Fill in Name (required), Company, Phone, Email, Interested plan, Expected value (₹) and Requirements.
  3. Press Add lead. It starts in New Lead.
Sample data

Name: Vistara Legal · Company: Vistara Legal LLP · Phone +91 90000 11122 · Email: office@vistara.example · Interested plan: Private Office · Expected value: ₹2,40,000 (12 months × ₹19,999 ≈ ₹2.4 lakh) · Requirements: Office for 5 lawyers, start 1 Nov, needs meeting room 10 h/month.

15.3 Moving a lead through the pipeline

  • Drag and drop a card to another column (computer).
  • Or use the small drop-down on the card to choose a stage (works well on phones).

The change is shown instantly. If saving fails, the card jumps back and an error appears. Every stage change is written into the lead’s timeline.

15.4 The lead drawer: notes, calls and timeline

Click a lead’s name to open a side panel with all its details and an Activity timeline.

  1. Choose Note or Call log, type what happened or the follow-up needed, and press Add to timeline.
  2. Entries are listed newest first with date and time. Stage moves appear automatically (“Moved from Contacted to Tour Scheduled”).
Sample timeline

When a lead is won

Moving a lead to Won does not create a member automatically. Add them as a member (chapter 7) and start their plan so they are invoiced. Won leads feed your lead conversion figure in Analytics.
