Chapter 11Invoices and GST

Every charge in Nexpreneur OS becomes a numbered, GST-ready tax invoice: plans, event tickets, services and anything you create by hand. This chapter explains the invoice list, creating invoices, how GST is split, and printing or emailing.

11.1 The invoice list

Sidebar › Invoices

Finance roles and managers see every invoice. Members and community managers see only their own. Columns: invoice number, customer, date, due date, total, balance and status.

StatusMeaning
UnpaidNothing paid yet and not past its due date.
Part paidSome money received; a balance remains.
PaidFully paid.
OverdueUnpaid or part paid and the due date has passed (worked out automatically each day).
VoidCancelled before any payment. Kept for the record; cannot be paid.

Invoice numbers are sequential per year with no gaps or duplicates: INV-2026-0001, INV-2026-0002… A new year restarts at 0001.

11.3 How GST is worked out

Your business is registered in Telangana. The system applies GST like this:

Customer locationTax shown on the invoice
Same state (Telangana)CGST + SGST, each half of the GST. At 18%: 9% + 9%.
Another state (tick “IGST”)IGST, the full GST. At 18%: 18%. The total is the same; only the split differs.

GST is calculated on the invoice as a whole and rounded to the nearest paisa; if the tax cannot be split evenly, the extra paisa goes to SGST so the lines always add up exactly.

SAC codes printed per line: plans and bookings use 997212 (rental of commercial property); event tickets 999699; services 998599. Your tax adviser can confirm the right codes for your case.

11.5 Discounts and company details on an invoice

When a coupon was used, the invoice shows a Discount (CODE) line between Subtotal and tax. When billed to a company, the company name and GSTIN appear above the contact person.

Chapter 12Payments, online payment and refunds

Money comes in two ways: members pay online through Razorpay, or staff record what was paid at the desk. Either way the invoice updates by itself.

12.1 The Payments page

Sidebar › Payments

A list of payments with date, invoice, customer, method, amount and status, plus an Outstanding card showing what is still owed. Members see only their own payments; finance roles see everyone’s.

12.2 Recording a payment at the desk

Invoice › Record payment
  1. Open the invoice.
  2. In Pay this invoice, set the Amount (it starts as the full balance, and cannot be more).
  3. Choose the Method: Cash, UPI, Bank transfer or Card. Add a Note such as a UTR number.
  4. Press Record payment.

The invoice moves to Part paid or Paid and the member gets a “Payment received” notification.

12.3 Paying online with Razorpay

When online payments are switched on (chapter 25), a Pay ₹… online button appears on unpaid invoices for the member and for staff.

  1. Press the button. The amount is the invoice balance as held by the system; it cannot be edited in the browser.
  2. The Razorpay window opens. Pay by UPI, card, netbanking or wallet.
  3. On success the invoice is marked paid within seconds, and a receipt notification is sent.
How it stays safe

Every online payment is verified three ways: Razorpay’s signature is checked, the system asks Razorpay directly what was paid, and a webhook from Razorpay confirms it again. A payment is recorded exactly once even if the member’s browser closes at the wrong moment.

If online payment is not switched on, the page says “Online payment isn’t enabled yet. Please pay at reception.”

12.4 Refunds

Invoice › Payments list › Refund

Owners, managers and finance can refund part or all of a payment.

  1. On the invoice, find the payment and press Refund.
  2. Set the amount (up to what has not been refunded yet) and an optional reason.
  3. Press Refund.
Payment methodWhat happens
Online (Razorpay)The refund is sent to Razorpay first and returns to the customer’s card or UPI. Only if Razorpay accepts is it recorded.
Cash, UPI, bank, card at the deskThe system records the refund; you hand the money back yourself.

The invoice’s paid amount goes down and its status updates (Paid → Part paid → Unpaid). The payment shows “refunded ₹…”, the customer gets a notification, and the action is written to the audit log.

Sample data

Aarav paid ₹4,718.82 by UPI; the desk is asked to refund ₹1,000 for an unused day pass. After the refund: payment shows refunded ₹1,000.00; the invoice shows Paid ₹3,718.82, Balance due ₹1,000.00, status Part paid. Refunding the remaining ₹3,718.82 later marks the payment refunded in full.

Careful

Refunds cannot be undone. Check the amount twice. To remove an unpaid invoice use Void instead; a voided invoice cannot have payments.

Chapter 13Automatic renewals and payment reminders

You do not have to remember renewal dates or chase payments. A daily job issues the next invoice for each active membership and nudges members who have not paid.

13.1 Renewal invoices

Each day the job looks at every active membership whose renewal date is within 3 days. For each one it:

  1. Issues a new invoice for the plan at today’s plan price + GST, describing the period (“Flexi membership renewal (2026-11-05 to 2026-12-05)”).
  2. Moves the membership’s renewal date forward by one billing cycle.
  3. Notifies the member (“Flexi renews on 2026-11-05. Your renewal invoice is ready”) and emails the invoice.

Paused and cancelled memberships are skipped. Running the job twice in a day never creates duplicates.

Sample data: Aarav’s timeline
DateWhat happens
5 OctFlexi starts. Invoice INV-2026-0001 (₹4,718.82), due 12 Oct. Renewal date set to 5 Nov.
2 NovDaily job: renewal date is 3 days away. Invoice for 5 Nov – 5 Dec issued and emailed, due 5 Nov. Renewal date becomes 5 Dec.
3 NovInvoice is due within 2 days: first payment reminder (email + notification).
6 NovNot paid and now overdue: second reminder (3 days after the first).
7 NovAarav pays online; reminders stop.

13.2 Payment reminders

Invoices that are unpaid or part paid get a reminder when they are due within 2 days or overdue. Rules:

  • At most one reminder every 3 days per invoice, and at most 5 in total, so nobody is nagged forever.
  • Each reminder is an in-app notification and, if email is set up, an email with a View & pay link.
  • Reminders stop as soon as the invoice is paid, voided or fully refunded.

13.3 Making the daily job run

The job runs when a scheduler calls a private web address once a day. Setup is a one-time task for the administrator (chapter 25.8). If the job is not scheduled, nothing breaks, but renewals and reminders do not happen automatically.

What the job reports

A successful call returns a short message such as {"ok":true,"renewals":{"issued":2,"errors":0},"reminders":{"sent":3,"errors":0}}. Zeros are normal on a quiet day.
