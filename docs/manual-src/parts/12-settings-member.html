Chapter 23Settings: team, roles and audit log

Settings is for owners. Here you decide who works in the system, what they can do, and you can see a record of what happened.

23.1 Team & roles

Sidebar › Settings › Team & roles

A table of everyone in the organisation (members and staff) with role, last sign-in and status.

Adding a team member

  1. Press Add team member.
  2. Enter name, email, choose a role (Reception, Finance, Community Manager, Staff, Location Manager, Owner) and type a temporary password (8 to 72 characters).
  3. Press Add member and share the password privately. The person changes it on their Profile page after signing in.

Changing a role or deactivating

  • Use the role drop-down on a person’s row. A confirmation warns that access changes immediately.
  • Deactivate blocks sign-in at once; Reactivate restores it. Nothing is deleted.
Safety ruleWhy
You cannot change your own role or deactivate yourself.Prevents locking yourself out. Ask another owner.
Only a Super Admin can grant or change the Super Admin role.Keeps the top role tightly held.
Changes take effect on the very next page the person opens.The role is checked against the database every time.

23.2 Audit log

The second tab lists the 60 most recent recorded actions, with who did them and when: sign-ins and sign-ups, bookings created or cancelled, invoices created, payments recorded, refunds, plan price changes, member and role changes, resources and locations changed, announcements sent and more. Use it to answer “who changed this?”.

Chapter 24The member guide (a one-page tour for members)

Print or share this chapter with your members. It covers everything a member does, in the order they will do it.

24.1 Your first day

  1. Set your password. Open the welcome email and press Set my password (the link lasts 7 days). If you were given a temporary password, sign in and change it under Profile.
  2. Sign in at os.nexpreneur.com with your password, an emailed link, or Google.
  3. Complete your profile: phone, job title, company, emergency contact. Your job title and company are shown in the member directory.
