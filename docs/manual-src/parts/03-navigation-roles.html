Chapter 3Finding your way around

A few tools appear on every page. Learning them first makes everything else quicker.

3.1 Sidebar and mobile navigation

The sidebar lists the sections your role may use. Items are in this order: Dashboard, Bookings, Calendar, Members, Companies, Memberships, Locations, Resources, Payments, Invoices, Visitors, CRM, Events, Community, Services, Analytics, AI Assistant, Notifications, Support, Settings, Profile. You only see the ones allowed for you.

  • Collapse (bottom of the sidebar) shrinks it to icons to give wide tables more room.
  • On a phone the sidebar is replaced by a bottom bar with five shortcuts. Members get Home, Bookings, Membership, Payments, Profile; staff get their first five sections. Everything else is reachable through Search.

3.2 The location selector

The box at the top left decides which location you are looking at. It affects Bookings, Resources, Members, Services orders, Analytics and the Dashboard.

If you chooseYou see
All Locations (owners only)Everything together.
Nexpreneur HyderabadOnly resources, bookings, memberships and invoices of that location.
Sample data

Members list with All Locations: 87 members. Switch to Nexpreneur Warangal: the list shows members whose home location is Warangal, plus members who have no home location set (they appear everywhere until you assign one).

Your choice is remembered on this device. Locations you add (chapter 6) appear in the selector immediately.

3.3 Search

Press Ctrl+K (⌘+K on a Mac) or press the search box. Type at least two letters.

  • Pages are matched first (type “inv” to jump to Invoices).
  • Records follow: members, resources, invoices (by number), events, leads, companies. You only see record types your role may open.
  • Press Enter to open the first result, or click any result. Esc closes.
Sample data

Type zen → Aarav Reddy · Member (company Zenith Labs)  |  type INV-2026 → every invoice from 2026  |  type room → Meeting Room A, Meeting Room B.

3.4 The notification bell

The bell shows how many notifications you have not read and updates about once a minute. Press it to open the Notification Centre (chapter 20).

3.5 Dark mode

The moon/sun button switches between light and dark mode. Pick whichever is easier on your eyes; invoices always print in black on white.

3.6 The AI button

The round button at the bottom right opens the AI Assistant (chapter 22). It is hidden on the Assistant page itself.

Money is protected

Roles without finance rights (reception, staff, community) never receive revenue, MRR, outstanding amounts, payments or renewal lists, even on the Dashboard. The figures are not simply hidden on screen; they are not sent to the browser at all.
