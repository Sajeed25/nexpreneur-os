Chapter 1Welcome to Nexpreneur OS

SECTIONSTART

Nexpreneur OS is one web application for running a whole coworking business: who your members are, what spaces they book, what they owe and have paid, who is visiting today, which leads are about to sign, and how the business is performing. It works on a laptop, tablet or phone, with nothing to install.

1.1 What you can do with it

AreaWhat it does
Members & CompaniesKeep a record of every member and company, with plan, location, contact details, GSTIN and what they owe.
Memberships & CouponsCreate plans (Flexi, Fixed Desk, Private Office, Virtual Office or your own), start them for members, pause or cancel them, and offer discount coupons.
BookingsBook hot desks, dedicated desks, meeting rooms, private offices, phone booths and event spaces, with live availability, a calendar and a floor map. Double-booking is not possible.
Invoices & PaymentsGST-ready tax invoices (CGST + SGST or IGST), online payment through Razorpay, cash/UPI/bank/card recorded at the desk, part payments, refunds, and e-mailed invoices.
Automatic renewals & remindersRenewal invoices are issued for you before each renewal date, and members get polite payment reminders.
VisitorsMembers invite guests and get a QR code to share; reception checks guests in and out.
CRMA sales pipeline for enquiries: new lead through tour, proposal and negotiation to won or lost.
Events & CommunityPublish events with photos and optional paid tickets; a member feed with posts, jobs, questions, comments and likes; a member directory.
ServicesA small marketplace (printing, parking, coffee, mail handling…) that members order and are invoiced for.
Analytics & DashboardRevenue, MRR/ARR, occupancy, churn, conversion, location comparison, and CSV export.
AI AssistantAsk in plain language: “Show available desks tomorrow”, “Book Meeting Room A at 3 PM”. It never books anything until you press Confirm.
Notifications, Support, SettingsAn in-app notification centre, a help-request inbox, and team & role management with a full audit log.

1.3 How to read this manual

  • Sidebar › Members › Add member shows the path to a screen or button.
  • An orange-bordered Sample data box is a worked example using the fictional business below. Follow along with your own details.
  • Tip boxes (green) save time. Note boxes (blue) explain how something works. Careful boxes (orange) prevent mistakes.
  • All amounts are in Indian rupees (₹). Times are India Standard Time (IST).

1.5 The screen at a glance

Every page has the same frame, so once you know it you know the whole product.

Part of the screenWhat it does
SidebarLists only the sections your role may use. The Collapse button at the bottom shrinks it to icons. On a phone it becomes a bottom bar.
Location selectorChooses which location you are looking at. Owners also get All Locations.
Search (Ctrl+K)Jump to any page, or find a member, invoice, resource, event, lead or company.
BellNotifications with an unread count.
Help (?)Opens search so you can jump straight to a page.
Moon / sunSwitches between light and dark mode (remembered on this device).
