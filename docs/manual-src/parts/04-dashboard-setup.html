Chapter 5The Dashboard and member Home

The Dashboard is your morning briefing: how busy the space is, how money is moving, and what needs attention today. Members get a simpler, personal Home instead.

5.1 The admin Dashboard

Sidebar › Dashboard

Pick a period with the tabs at the top right: Today, 7 Days, 30 Days, 90 Days or This Year. Everything on the page, and the location selector, follows your choice.

CardMeaning
Revenue collectedPayments received in the period, including GST. (Finance roles only.)
MRRMonthly recurring revenue: what active plans bring in per month, before GST. A yearly plan counts as one twelfth. (Finance roles only.)
Active membersPeople with an active plan.
OccupancyBooked hours ÷ open hours (06:00–22:00) across all your resources.
BookingsConfirmed and completed bookings that start in the period.
OutstandingUnpaid invoice balances. (Finance roles only.)

Below the cards are four charts (Revenue trend, Occupancy trend, Member growth, Booking trends) and up to six lists:

ListShows
Today’s activityThe latest actions in the system, in plain words: “Divya Nair checked a visitor in”.
Upcoming bookingsThe next five confirmed bookings.
Recent paymentsThe latest five payments received. (Finance roles.)
Upcoming eventsNext published events.
New leadsLatest CRM leads with expected value. (Roles with CRM.)
Membership renewalsThe next renewal dates. (Finance roles.)
Note

Chart buckets change with the period: hourly for Today, daily for 7 and 30 days, weekly for 90 days, monthly for This Year.

5.2 Member Home

Members see a friendly page instead:

  • Greeting that follows the time of day (Good morning / afternoon / evening).
  • Upcoming booking: the next confirmed booking, or a link to book a space.
  • Quick actions: Book Desk, Meeting Room, Day Pass, Invite Visitor, Events.
  • Membership card: plan, price and renewal date.
  • Outstanding: how much they owe, with a link to pay.

Chapter 6Setting up your space: Locations and Resources

Before anyone can book, the system needs to know what you have: your locations, and every desk, room and office inside them with its price.

6.1 Locations

Sidebar › Locations

Each location is a card showing the number of resources and active members. Owners can:

  • Add location: enter a name (for example “Nexpreneur Karimnagar”) and a city. It appears in the top selector at once.
  • Edit: rename it or change the city.
  • Archive: hides a location you no longer use. The system refuses while the location still has resources or active members, so nothing is left pointing at a closed space. You always need at least one location.

6.2 Resources: what you rent out

Sidebar › Resources

Resources are the things people book. Each has a type, a location, a capacity, a price and a status.

TypeChargedTypical example

All prices are before 18% GST; GST is added when someone books.

Adding resources (owners and managers)

  1. Press Add resource.
  2. Type a name, choose the location and type, set capacity and the price (per hour or per day depending on the type) and the status.
  3. To create many at once, set How many to add. The system numbers them for you.
  4. Press Add.
Sample data

Name: Hot Desk H  ·  Location: Nexpreneur Hyderabad  ·  Type: Hot Desk  ·  Capacity: 1  ·  Price per day: ₹300  ·  How many: 16

Result: sixteen desks named Hot Desk H 01 to Hot Desk H 16, each ₹300 per day.

Status and archiving

StatusEffect
AvailableCan be booked.
ReservedHeld (for example for a long-term tenant). Not bookable.
MaintenanceTemporarily out of use. Not bookable. Use this instead of archiving when a room is only being repaired.
UnavailableNot bookable.

Change a status straight from the table with the drop-down. Archive removes a resource from booking and the floor map. It is blocked if the resource has upcoming bookings (cancel those first).

Changing a price

Press Edit, change the price, Save changes. New bookings use the new price; bookings already made keep the price they were made at.

Tip: sample resources

If the Floor map tab in Bookings is empty, owners will see an Add demo resources button that creates about 30 desks and rooms across your locations to explore with. You can archive them later.
