Chapter 8Memberships, plans and coupons

A plan is what you sell (for example Flexi at ₹3,999 a month). A membership is one member being on one plan. This chapter covers both, plus discount coupons.

8.1 Plans

Sidebar › Memberships

Plans appear as cards at the top of the page showing the name, price, billing cycle, benefits and (for managers) how many members are active on it. Prices are before 18% GST.

8.2 Creating and changing plans (owners and managers)

New plan

  1. Press New plan.
  2. Enter the plan name, price in rupees (before GST), choose a billing cycle (Monthly, Quarterly or Yearly) and list benefits, one per line.
  3. Press Create plan.

Changing a price or benefits

On the plan card press Edit price & details, change what you need, and Save.

Important: what a price change affects

A new price applies to invoices issued from now on, including automatic renewals. Invoices already issued never change. People already on the plan simply pay the new price from their next renewal.

Archiving a plan

Archive stops a plan being offered to new members. People already on it stay on it and keep renewing. Restore brings it back. Archived plans are shown faded.

Sample data

Create Startup Desk with price ₹17,999 and cycle Quarterly. The price is for the whole cycle, so each member is invoiced ₹17,999 + GST every 3 months. Benefits: dedicated desk, 10 meeting-room hours, 2 visitor passes per month.

Later you raise Flexi to ₹4,299: Aarav’s next renewal invoice is ₹4,299 + GST; his October invoice (₹4,718.82) stays as it was.

8.3 Assigning a membership

Memberships › Assign membership   or   Member page › Membership

Owners, managers and finance can start a plan for any member.

FieldNotes
MemberPick from your people.
PlanShows the price next to each plan.
Start dateDefaults to today. The renewal date is the start date plus one billing cycle.
LocationDefaults to the location chosen in the top selector. Used for location reports.
Coupon codeOptional (8.5).
Email the invoiceTicked by default. Sends the invoice to the member if email is set up.

Press Start membership & issue invoice. In one step the system creates the membership, issues invoice INV-YYYY-NNNN (plan price + 18% GST, due in 7 days) and tells the member through a notification.

Sample data

Member Aarav Reddy · Plan Flexi · Start 5 Oct 2026 · Location Hyderabad.

  • Renewal date: 5 Nov 2026
  • Invoice INV-2026-0001: ₹3,999.00 + CGST ₹359.91 + SGST ₹359.91 = ₹4,718.82, due 12 Oct 2026
Month-end dates

Renewal dates never fall on a day that does not exist. A plan started on 31 January renews on 28 February (29 in a leap year), and then on 28 March.

8.4 Pausing and cancelling

In the memberships table (and on the member’s page) use the buttons on each row:

ButtonEffect
PauseStatus becomes paused. No renewal invoices are issued. History is kept.
ResumeStatus returns to active. The existing renewal date is kept; if it has already passed, the next daily run issues the invoice.
CancelAsks you to confirm, then ends the plan for good (cancelled). The date is recorded and counted in churn reports. To restart, assign a new plan.

8.5 Coupons

Memberships › Coupons

Coupons give discounts on invoices. Owners and managers create them; anyone who creates an invoice, starts a plan or orders a service can type a code.

FieldRules
Code3 to 40 letters, numbers, - or _. Capitalised automatically. Must be unique.
TypePercent off (a whole number from 1 to 100) or Fixed ₹ off.
ValueThe percentage, or the rupee amount.
Max usesOptional. Once reached, the coupon stops working. Two people cannot both use the last one.
Valid untilOptional last day. After it, the coupon is refused.

The list shows each coupon with how many times it has been used. Disable / Enable switches it off or on without deleting history.

How the discount is calculated

The discount comes off the price before GST, and GST is charged on what is left. This is the correct treatment for a discount given at the time of invoicing, and the invoice shows both.

Worked example: Flexi ₹3,999 with two coupons
StepWELCOME10 (10% off)FLAT500 (₹500 off)
Price before GST₹3,999.00₹3,999.00
Discount− ₹399.90− ₹500.00
Taxable value₹3,599.10₹3,499.00
GST 18%₹647.84₹629.82
Total payable₹4,246.94₹4,128.82
Tip

Make launch offers time-limited and use-limited: WELCOME10 with Max uses 100 and Valid until 31 Dec 2026. You will see exactly how many members took it.

Chapter 9Companies

Many members belong to a business. A company record lets you keep its GSTIN and billing address, link its people, see what it owes, and bill it on one GST invoice.

9.1 The Companies page

Sidebar › Companies

Owners, managers and finance see a card for each company: name, GSTIN, billing email, address, members linked to it, and Outstanding (the total of its unpaid invoices).

9.2 Adding and editing a company

  1. Press New company.
  2. Fill in Company name (required), GSTIN (15 characters, optional), Phone, Billing email and Billing address.
  3. Press Save company.
Sample data

Edit changes any detail. Delete removes the company from the list and unlinks its members; their accounts and all past invoices are untouched.

9.3 Linking members

Inside a company card, the Add a member… menu lists people who are not yet in it. Choose one to link them; press Remove beside a name to unlink. You can also pick the company when adding or editing a member (chapter 7).

9.4 Billing a company

When you create an invoice (chapter 11) choose Bill to company. The company name and GSTIN appear on the invoice above the contact person, and the company’s Outstanding total updates automatically.

Sample data

Zenith Labs has two members: Aarav Reddy and Priya Sharma. You create one invoice for 2 Flexi desks (2 × ₹3,999 = ₹7,998 + GST ₹1,439.64 = ₹9,437.64), choose Bill to company: Zenith Labs Pvt Ltd. Zenith’s card now shows Outstanding ₹9,437.64.
