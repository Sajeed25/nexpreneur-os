Chapter 16Events

Workshops, meetups, demo days: publish an event with a photo, set a capacity and optional ticket price, and let members register in one tap. Paid tickets create a GST invoice automatically.

16.1 Creating an event (owners, managers, community managers)

Sidebar › Events › Create event
FieldNotes
Title3 to 160 characters.
DescriptionUp to 2,000 characters.
Date, Starts, EndsIndia time, in 30-minute steps. End must be after start. Creating an event in the past is refused.
VenueFor example “Event Space, Hyderabad”.
CapacityMaximum number of registrations.
Ticket priceRupees before GST. 0 means free.
OrganizerName shown on the event.
Event imageOptional JPEG, PNG or WebP. Photos are shrunk automatically to a sensible size (largest side 1,200 pixels) before upload.
Publish nowTick to show it to members. Untick to keep it as a draft.
Sample data

Title: Founders Breakfast · Friday 9 Oct 2026, 09:00 – 11:00 · Venue: Event Space, Hyderabad · Capacity 40 · Ticket ₹500 (+ GST) · Organizer: Sneha Iyer · Image: photo of the space · Published.

Ticket cost to a member: ₹500 + 18% GST (₹90) = ₹590.

16.2 Managing events

Each event card shows the image, date and time, venue, registered / capacity and price. Managers have buttons:

ButtonWhat it does
EditOpens the form filled in. You can change anything. Capacity cannot go below the number already registered. A new price applies to people who register from now on.
Publish / UnpublishShows or hides the event from members. Drafts are labelled in grey.
AttendeesLists the name and email of everyone registered.

16.3 Registering as a member

  1. Open Events and find the event.
  2. Press Register (free) or Register & get invoice (paid).
  3. For a paid event, a green message links to your ticket invoice. Open it and pay online. The invoice is due in 3 days.

The card then says You’re going. To change your mind press Cancel registration (before the event starts); your seat is released. If the event is full the button reads Full.

Capacity is exact

The system locks the event while it counts, so two people registering for the last seat at the same moment cannot both get it.

17.2 Member directory

Community › Member directory

A searchable list of everyone in the space: name, role and company only. Emails and phone numbers are never shown here. Keep your own entry useful by filling in job title and company on your Profile.

18.2 Ordering (members)

  1. Open Services.
  2. On the service, set the quantity (1 to 50) and optionally a coupon.
  3. Press Order. A green message links to your invoice.

The price always comes from the system, never from the browser. The order appears under Your orders.

Sample data

Aarav orders 50 pages of colour printing: 50 × ₹10 = ₹500 + GST ₹90 = ₹590, invoice INV-2026-0009, due in 7 days.

Chapter 19Support requests

A simple inbox so members can ask for help (“AC not working in Meeting Room A”) and your team can see and close requests.

Sidebar › Support

19.1 For members

  1. Write a short Subject (3 to 160 characters) and the Details (5 to 2,000).
  2. Press Send request. It appears below as Open.
  3. When it is sorted, you are notified. You can also press Mark as closed yourself.

19.2 For owners, managers and reception

You see every request with the member’s name and time. Press Mark as closed when done; the member gets a notification. Closed requests stay in the list as Closed for the record.

Note

Support requests do not currently have a back-and-forth reply thread. Reply to the member in person, by phone, or by email, then close the request.
