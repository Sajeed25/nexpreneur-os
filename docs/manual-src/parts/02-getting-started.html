2.2 Creating the Owner account

Open › os.nexpreneur.com/register
  1. Type your full name, email and a password (8 to 72 characters).
  2. Press Create account.
  3. You are signed in as Owner. In the background the system created your organisation Nexpreneur, three locations (Hyderabad, Warangal, Nalgonda) and four plans (Flexi ₹3,999, Fixed Desk ₹4,999, Private Office ₹19,999, Virtual Office ₹999 per month).
Sample data

Name: Mohammed Sajeed   Email: owner@nexpreneur.example   Password: a long phrase you will remember, such as Coworking-Hyd-2026!

Note

Anyone who registers after the Owner joins as a Member with no extra rights. Owners promote people to staff roles in Settings (chapter 23). You can also add members yourself (chapter 7), which is how most coworking spaces work.

2.3 Signing in

Open › os.nexpreneur.com/login

Option A: email and password

Type your email and password and press Sign in. After 8 wrong attempts in 10 minutes the system pauses sign-in for a few minutes to stop password guessing.

Option B: email me a link

  1. On the sign-in page press Email me a link and enter your email.
  2. Open the email and press the link. A page opens with a Sign in button.
  3. Press it. You are in. The link works once and expires after 15 minutes.
Why the extra button?

Some email programs open links automatically to scan them. The extra click makes sure a scanner cannot use up your one-time link before you do.

Option C: Google

Press Google, pick your Google account, and you are signed in. If an account with the same verified email exists, you enter that account. Otherwise a new Member account is created.

Note

The Email link and Google buttons are greyed out until an administrator sets up email and Google (chapter 25). Password sign-in always works.

2.4 Forgotten password

  1. On the sign-in page press Forgot password? and enter your email.
  2. You always see “If an account exists for that email, we’ve sent a reset link”. This is deliberate: it stops strangers checking who has an account.
  3. Open the email, press the link, choose a new password twice, and press Update password.
  4. Sign in with the new password. All your other devices are signed out for safety.

The link works once and lasts one hour. If email is not set up yet, the page says so; ask an owner or reception to set a temporary password for you (chapter 7.6).

2.6 Demo mode

If the system has no database connected yet, the sign-in page shows a Sign in as menu and any email and password work. This is demo mode: it shows sample screens so you can look around, but nothing is saved. As soon as a real database is connected, demo sessions stop working and the menu disappears.

2.7 Staying signed in and signing out

  • A session lasts 8 hours, then you sign in again.
  • Your role is checked against the database on every page. If an owner changes your role or deactivates your account, it takes effect immediately.
  • To leave: avatar › Sign out. On a shared computer always sign out.
