Important things reach you without you having to look for them. Notifications appear under the bell at the top of every page and in a full list.

20.2 Using it

  • The bell shows the number of unread items (and “9+” if more) and refreshes about once a minute while the page is open.
  • Open Notifications to see the list, newest first. Unread items have a tinted background and an orange dot.
  • Click an item to open what it is about (an invoice, your bookings, events…) and mark it read. Mark all as read clears the bell.
Note

Notifications update about every minute rather than instantly. Email copies are sent for invoices, reminders and visitor invitations when email is set up.

20.3 Sending an announcement

Owners, managers and community managers see Send an announcement on this page. Enter a title and optional details and press Send to everyone. Every active person in the organisation, members and staff, gets a notification; the screen tells you how many.

Sample data

Title: Hyderabad: power maintenance on Sunday 11 Oct · Details: The building will be closed 8 AM – 2 PM. Desks resume Monday. → “Announcement sent to 94 people.”

Chapter 21Analytics and reports

Know how the business is doing: money, space use, members and sales. Available to owners, managers and finance. You can export everything to a spreadsheet.

21.1 Using the page

Sidebar › Analytics
  • Choose a period: 7 Days, 30 Days, 90 Days or This Year.
  • The location selector filters the page. Choose All Locations for the whole business.
  • Export CSV downloads the numbers as a spreadsheet file (opens in Excel or Google Sheets), named for the period and location.

21.2 The twelve numbers, in plain words

NumberHow it is worked out
Revenue collectedAll payments received in the period, including GST.
MRRMonthly recurring revenue: for every active membership, the plan price per month before GST (quarterly ÷ 3, yearly ÷ 12), added up.
ARRAnnual run-rate: MRR × 12.
Active membersPeople who currently have an active plan.
New membersPeople who joined in the period (for one location: whose first plan there started in the period).
ChurnPlans that ended in the period ÷ (active plans + plans that ended). A cancelled plan counts on its cancellation date.
BookingsConfirmed and completed bookings starting in the period.
OccupancyBooked hours ÷ open hours (06:00–22:00 every day) of all resources in the period.
Room utilizationThe same, for meeting rooms only.
Avg revenue / memberMRR ÷ active members.
Lead conversionLeads marked Won ÷ leads created in the period. Organisation-wide.
Membership conversionPeople who have ever had a plan ÷ all members. Organisation-wide.
Worked example: a 30-day report for all locations

In this example the business has 20 bookable resources, of which 4 are meeting rooms.

NumberWorkingResult
MRR40 × ₹3,999 + 22 × ₹4,999 + 5 × ₹19,999 + 20 × ₹999₹3,89,913

21.3 Charts and the location table

  • Revenue (area), Occupancy % (line), Member growth (area, total members at the end of each period), Bookings (bars), Churn (bars of plans ended).
  • Chart buckets: daily for 7 and 30 days, weekly for 90 days, monthly for This Year.
  • Location performance: for each location the bookings, booking revenue and occupancy. This table always compares every location, whichever one you selected.

21.4 Reading the numbers wisely

Things to remember
  • Occupancy counts booked hours, not seats filled. A booked meeting room counts for every hour it is booked even with one person in it.
  • Members who pay by plan and never book a space still count as active members but add nothing to occupancy.
  • Revenue collected includes GST; MRR does not. They are not meant to match.
  • Data created before the location filters existed was assigned to your first location when the system was upgraded.

The CSV file

The export contains three blocks: the twelve numbers, the time series (period by period), and the location table. Text that begins with =, +, - or @ is neutralised so a spreadsheet can never run it as a formula.

Chapter 22The AI Assistant

Ask in everyday words. The assistant looks things up in your real data, and prepares bookings for you to confirm. It never books, cancels or pays anything on its own.

22.1 Opening it

Use AI Assistant in the sidebar, or the round button at the bottom right of any page. Members and owners/managers can use it. Tap a suggestion chip or type your own question.

22.2 What it can do

You sayIt does
“Show available desks tomorrow”Checks availability and prices for that day.
“Show my invoices”Lists your recent invoices with totals and status.
“How much is my membership?”Tells you your plan, renewal date and all plan prices.
“What events are happening this week?”Lists upcoming published events, price and spots left.
“Book a meeting room tomorrow at 3 PM”Asks anything missing, then prepares a booking for you to confirm.
“Cancel my booking”Finds your upcoming bookings and prepares a cancellation for you to confirm.

It sees only what your role may see: a member’s assistant shows that member’s own invoices and bookings.

22.3 A booking conversation, step by step

Book Meeting Room A tomorrow at 3 PM
Checking availability… Room A is free tomorrow from 3 PM. Which location, and how long do you need? You can say 1, 2 or 3 hours.
Nexpreneur Hyderabad, 2 hours
Meeting Room A is available 15:00 – 17:00. I’ve prepared the booking. Please confirm below.

Done. Your booking is confirmed (₹1,888 incl. GST, payable at reception).

The card’s title, time and price are produced by the system from real data, not by the AI’s wording.

22.4 Safety built in

  • Nothing happens until you press Confirm. The confirmation lasts 10 minutes and works only for the person it was made for. Editing it in the browser makes it fail.
  • When you confirm, the normal booking checks run again: availability, opening hours and permissions.
  • The assistant cannot take payments, change prices or edit anything else. For payment it points you to Invoices.
  • Instructions hidden inside data (for example in an event description) are ignored.
  • Each person can send up to 30 messages every 10 minutes.
If it says it is unavailable

Owners see the real reason. The most common one is OPENAI_429: the OpenAI account has no credit. API credit is billed separately from a ChatGPT subscription (chapter 26).
